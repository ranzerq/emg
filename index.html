Motor effect & Fleming's left-hand rule

Rule: hold your left hand with thumb, first finger and second finger at right angles. First finger = Field (N→S), seCond finger = Current (+ → −), Thumb = Thrust (force / motion).

A wire carrying current in a magnetic field feels a force. The two sides of the coil carry current in opposite directions, so the forces are opposite and make a turning effect.
